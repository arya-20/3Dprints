Good to know

How ordering works
  1. Add prints to your order and pick a color for each.
  2. Send the order. It reaches me by email.
  3. I reply with the final price, shipping and timing.
  4. You pay using the details I send, then I print and ship.
Materials and finish

Most prints use PLA: rigid, matte and fine for indoor use. PETG is available for outdoor spots or anywhere warm.

Standard layer height is 0.2 mm. Ask if you want a finer finish.

Shipping and turnaround

Most orders print within 3 to 5 days. Shipping is quoted per order once I know the weight.

Custom prints take longer, and I will tell you how long before you commit.
